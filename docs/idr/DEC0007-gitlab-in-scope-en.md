## Context

The specification listed GitLab among the platforms not supported, with Bitbucket and Gerrit, providing for extensibility without implementing it (§9.2).

## Decision

GitLab enters the product's scope, as its third target platform.

## Rationale

The original text records no rationale beyond the decision. The annex C that comes with it is built on the same frame as annexes A and B: what the gitlab.com captures measured, what GitLab's documentation says (cited), and "to verify" everywhere else.

## Alternatives considered

No alternative was documented when the decision was made.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The product targets three platforms.

### Negative

- None recorded when the decision was made.

### Risks

- None recorded when the decision was made.

### Follow-up actions

- Bitbucket and Gerrit stay out of scope.
